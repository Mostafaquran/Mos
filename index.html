Meet Your Quran Teacher

My name is Mostafa Mahmoud, and I provide online Quran teaching and memorization lessons specially designed for non-Arab Muslims.

My goal is to help Muslim students build a strong relationship with the Holy Quran by teaching them how to read it correctly, improve their pronunciation, learn Tajweed, memorize Quranic verses and Surahs, and continuously revise what they have memorized.

Our Programs

Quran Learning Services

Comprehensive lessons customized for your pace, level, and goals.

Quran Memorization

Step-by-step Quran memorization lessons designed according to the student's ability and available study time.

Students receive a clear memorization and revision plan to help them memorize gradually and consistently.

Quran Reading for Beginners

Helping non-Arab Muslims who have difficulty reading Arabic to gradually learn how to read Quranic words and verses correctly.

Highly suitable for beginners who are just starting their Quran learning journey.

Quran Recitation

Students practice reading Quran directly with the teacher while mistakes in pronunciation and recitation are corrected gently in real-time.

Tajweed Lessons

Teaching the essential Tajweed rules needed to recite the Holy Quran correctly and beautifully.

Rules are explained in a very simple and practical way suitable for non-Arab students.

Pronunciation Correction

Special attention is given to correctly pronouncing Arabic letters (Makharij) and Quranic words.

Especially useful for non-Arab Muslims who struggle with specific Arabic sounds.

Memorization Revision

Regular revision sessions designed to help students strengthen previously memorized Surahs and prevent forgetting them over time.

Surah Memorization

Choose to memorize specific Surahs, short Surahs, Juz Amma, or gradually work toward memorizing larger portions of the Quran.

Al-Fatiha Correction

Special focused lessons to make sure you are reciting Surah Al-Fatiha correctly, which is essential for daily prayers.
